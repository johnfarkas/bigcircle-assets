The mark, briefly

Six members, each with its own color and its own angle onto the same center, held inside one unbroken circle. The space they leave between them resolves into a fan turning the same way they do.

One circle

A continuous ring of constant weight. It draws the boundary of the auction, and membership is what it encloses.

Six members

Identical in size and weight, entering from six angles 60° apart. The circle closes when all six are present.

Clockwise

The members turn in the direction of rounds advancing toward Best & Final. The mark is never mirrored or reversed in direction.

Built from circles

Every edge is a circle or a piece of one, placed by measurement on a 100-unit square.
