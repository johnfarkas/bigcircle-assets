Logo

The horizontal lockup is the primary form. Use the stacked lockup where the space is squarer than it is wide, and the icon alone only where the name is already present.

Horizontal lockup

The default. BIG CIRCLE sits on the mark’s centerline with AUCTIONS hanging below it.

Stacked lockup

The wordmark is sized so its width matches the mark exactly. Use it for profile images, app tiles, and anywhere the space is roughly square.

Icon

For favicons, app icons, and avatars. Holds to 32 px in full color, 24 px in single ink.

Favicons. The SVG scales to every size and is what most browsers use. PNGs are provided for iOS home screens and web app manifests.

favicon.svg 32 px 180 px · iOS 512 px · manifest

Usage

Full color

The default. It needs white or a very light ground to hold its edges.

Reversed on dark

The full palette with a white ring and wordmark. For ink grounds, dark photography, and the footer of a dark page.

All ink

One-color printing, embroidery, engraving, and any ground too busy for six colors. A genuinely single-color file.

All white

On ink, on photography, and on any of the six colors. Never place it on a light ground.

A note on the SVGs. The lockups carry the wordmark as live text in Archivo. Each text element sets its true measured width, so a substitute font fills the same box rather than reflowing — but for anything going to print, install Archivo or use the PNG. Outlined SVG and vector EPS are available on request.
