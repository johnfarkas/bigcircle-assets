Typography

Familjen Grotesk carries display, IBM Plex Sans carries every working word. Both are open source under the SIL Open Font License, so they can be installed, embedded, and sent to a vendor at no cost. The wordmark is drawn artwork — set it from the logo files, never from a font.

Familjen Grotesk

SemiBold 600 and Bold 700. Headlines, section titles, card titles, and any large figure. Squared terminals give it a slight engineering flavor that echoes the flywheel behind the mark, while staying clearly separate from the wordmark.
